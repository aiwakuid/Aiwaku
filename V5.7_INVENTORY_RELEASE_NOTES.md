# AIWAKU V5.7 — Recipe + Inventory Intelligence

## Goal
Move inventory from finished-menu stock toward ingredient-aware restaurant operations without making the UI complicated.

## Added
- Ingredient inventory with unit, stock, reorder point, and cost per unit.
- Recipe mapping per menu and ingredient quantity per serving.
- Atomic recipe consumption after order creation; insufficient ingredient stock aborts the transaction.
- Ingredient restoration when an order is cancelled.
- Inventory transaction ledger for sale/restock/return.
- Simple restock RPC with row locking.
- Food-cost estimate in the Persediaan screen.
- Low-stock signal and one-click restock flow.

## Important design rule
Menu stock remains supported for backward compatibility. Recipes are additive: only menus with configured recipes consume ingredient inventory.

## Next
Purchasing/supplier workflow, multi-location inventory, waste/adjustment, and AI reorder suggestions.
